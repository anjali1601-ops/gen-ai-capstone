Case overview: Customer James Okonkwo reported a delivery issue regarding a mixer that was delayed and arrived with damaged packaging. The mixer was expected on 15 March but was delivered on 18 March.
Key issue: The main concerns are the delay in delivery and the crushed carton upon arrival, which has caused dissatisfaction.
Action taken: The delivery partner noted that the delay was due to weather conditions, but no compensation or replacement packaging was provided to the customer.
Current status: The case is currently in progress and awaiting further resolution.
Recommended next action: Contact the customer to address their concerns and discuss possible compensation options.
